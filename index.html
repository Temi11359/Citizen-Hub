About the LCDA

Isheri-Olofin Local Council Development Area (LCDA) is dedicated to bringing governance closer to the people. We focus on enhancing community development, infrastructural growth, education, public health, and socioeconomic empowerment for all residents.

Our mission is to foster a safe, sustainable, and prosperous environment through participatory governance and progressive public administration.
